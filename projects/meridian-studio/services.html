What we do

From the first sketch to the final detail.

We work as one studio across architecture and interiors, so a project never has to change hands halfway through. Take all of it, or only the part you need.

i

Residential Architecture

New houses, extensions, and whole-house remodels, taken from a blank site to a finished building. We lead the design and coordinate every consultant, so the idea you agreed at the start is the one that gets built.

  • Feasibility & site appraisal
  • Concept design
  • Planning applications
  • Technical drawings
  • Tender & builder selection
  • Construction oversight
ii

Interior Design

Interiors resolved with the same rigour as the architecture: plan, joinery, materials, lighting, and the last layer of furniture. Whether alongside a build or on its own, the rooms are designed to be lived in, not just looked at.

  • Space planning
  • Bespoke joinery
  • Materials & finishes
  • Lighting design
  • Furniture & fittings
  • Styling & handover
iii

Renovations & Extensions

Careful work on older and listed homes. We keep what is worth keeping, repair what has been lost, and add what the house needs to suit the way people live now, all without losing its character.

  • Heritage & listed advice
  • Structural coordination
  • Extension design
  • Repair & restoration
  • Building regulations
  • Phased delivery
iv

Consultation

A focused way to get an architect's read before you commit. We visit, look hard at the site or the brief, and give you a clear, written sense of what is possible and what it is likely to cost.

  • Half-day site visit
  • Brief & feasibility review
  • Budget guidance
  • Design direction
  • Written summary

Ways to work together

Three ways to engage the studio.

We keep fees plain and agree them in writing before any work begins. No surprises, on either side.

Full commission

Concept to completion.

We lead the whole project, charged as a percentage of construction cost. Best for new houses and substantial renovations.

Defined stages

A fixed fee per stage.

Engage us stage by stage, each with a clear scope and price. Useful when you want to start small and decide as you go.

Consultation

A day rate, paid up front.

A single visit and written advice. The fee is credited against a full commission if you decide to go ahead within six months.
