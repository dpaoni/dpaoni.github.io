Selected work, 2021 to today.

Each project is numbered as it enters the studio. A house, a flat, a barn, a building on a hillside; different in scale, alike in care.

Saltmarsh House

Suffolk coast · In progress

A retirement house on the edge of the marshes, raised on a brick plinth and clad in silvered timber, planned to take in the long horizon.

New build Nº 017 · 2024

Tideline House

Greenwich, London

A family house set against the river in handmade brick and oak, planned to follow the north light and open fully to the water in summer.

Interiors Nº 015 · 2023

Wigmore Apartment

A mansion flat returned to its proportions, then layered with quiet colour, bespoke joinery, and lighting that flatters the evenings.

Slate Yard

Snowdonia, Wales

A working artist's studio and guest house in local stone and slate, set quietly into the hillside and built almost entirely by hand.

Notting Hill, London
