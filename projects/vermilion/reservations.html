Arriving & Parking

Vermilion sits on South Sixth Street in downtown Springfield, two blocks from the Old State Capitol and within easy reach of the city's principal hotels.

Complimentary valet parking is offered every evening at our Sixth Street entrance — simply present your vehicle to the attendant on arrival. Metered street parking and the adjacent public garage are also available for those who prefer to park themselves.

Should you require directions or wish to arrange a particular arrival, our team is glad to assist by telephone at (217) 555-1924.
