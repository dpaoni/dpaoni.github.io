The Menu

Prime beef, dry-aged in-house, and served by candlelight.

Every cut we serve is USDA Prime — the top two percent of American beef — selected by hand and dry-aged in our own cellar for depth and tenderness. Our kitchen sources from the season first: produce at its peak, seafood landed daily, and a cellar built across a century. What follows is offered simply, and changes as the market does.
