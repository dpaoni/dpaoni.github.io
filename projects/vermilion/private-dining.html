Private Dining

Private Rooms & Celebrations

Hosting the occasions that deserve a room of their own.

An Occasion Apart

For a century, the most consequential evenings in Springfield have unfolded beneath our candlelight. Rehearsal dinners and silver-anniversary toasts, board retreats and quiet homecomings — VERMILION has set the table for them all since 1924, with the same discretion that once welcomed senators through its doors.

Our private rooms carry the warmth of the main dining room and the privacy your gathering deserves. Tell us the occasion; we will tend to the rest.

Hosted With Care

A century of gracious hosting

From the first pour to the final toast, a dedicated maître d' attends to your evening. Menus are printed with your occasion at the header; wines are chosen to suit the room and the company. Nothing is left to chance, and nothing feels rehearsed.

Whether you are ten or one hundred and twenty, the standard is unchanged: prime, dry-aged, and served by candlelight.

Our Rooms

The Spaces

Four settings, each with its own character — chosen to match the size and spirit of your occasion.

Up to 40 Seated

The Cellar Room

Surrounded by the reserve wine collection, the Cellar Room is our most atmospheric setting — stone, candlelight, and bottles that span generations. Ideal for celebratory dinners that linger long past the last course.

Up to 16 Guests

The Lincoln Salon

Intimate and panelled, the Lincoln Salon opens onto its own private entrance off Sixth Street. A discreet choice for milestone birthdays, anniversaries, and the conversations meant for fewer ears.

Up to 10 Guests

The Boardroom

Fully AV-equipped and shielded from the dining room, the Boardroom is built for working dinners — presentations and negotiations conducted over prime steak rather than catered trays.

Up to 120 Guests

Full Buyout

For grand occasions, the entire dining room becomes yours alone. The full kitchen, the cellar, and our complete staff at your service for an evening of unhurried splendor.

The Setting

Rooms shaped by their wine

Our cellar holds more than four thousand selections, and the rooms that border it inherit its calm. Bottles can be brought to the table for your party, decanted at your pace, and paired course by course with our sommelier in attendance.

Curated for the Table

Group Menus & Pairings

Three ways to dine together — each prepared in advance so your guests are served as one.

Prix Fixe

The Founder's Menu

A four-course progression of our signatures — from the chilled tower to a dry-aged centerpiece — composed by the kitchen and finished tableside. The simplest path to a flawless evening.

Family Style

The Vermilion Table

Platters of prime cuts, seasonal sides, and shared starters passed among the company. Generous, convivial, and well suited to celebrations where conversation matters most.

Sommelier Led

Bespoke Wine Pairings

Drawn from our reserve cellar, a flight composed for your party and your menu. Verticals, library vintages, and large-format bottles arranged in advance with our wine director.
