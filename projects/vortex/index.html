About Us

Springfield's Property Care Crew

Vortex Property Services is a locally-owned residential and commercial property care company serving Springfield, IL and the surrounding area. We handle lawn care, power washing, and snow removal — all year, one crew.

We built Vortex on a simple idea: show up on time, communicate clearly, and leave the property better than we found it. No surprise invoices, no revolving door of strangers. Just reliable work from people who take it seriously.
