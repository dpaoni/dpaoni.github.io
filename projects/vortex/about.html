About Us

Built on showing up.

Vortex Property Services is a locally-owned residential and commercial property care company serving Springfield, IL and the surrounding area. We handle lawn care, power washing, and snow removal — all year, one crew.

We started Vortex because we kept hearing the same story: contractors who don't show up, estimates that change at the invoice, and service that stops the moment you stop following up. We built this company to be the opposite of that.

Every job gets a straight estimate upfront, consistent communication throughout, and a crew that takes pride in the finished result. We're not a franchise or a platform — we're a local business that depends on word of mouth, which means your experience matters to us every single time.

What we stand for

Reliability

We show up when we say we will. If anything changes, you hear from us first, not after the fact.

Transparency

Flat estimates, no hidden fees. You know the price before we start and it doesn't change at the end.

Quality

We don't rush through jobs to move on to the next one. The standard is simple: leave it better than we found it.

Local

Springfield-based, Springfield-focused. We live and work in the same community we serve.
