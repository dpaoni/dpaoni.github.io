A bit about me.

Springfield-based cybersecurity builder focused on Flipper Zero firmware, GPIO modules, and practical hardware security.

I'm a recent graduate of Capital Area Career Center's High Tech Engineering and Cybersecurity program in Springfield, IL. That mix of cyber, robotics, machining, and networking pushed me toward hardware security instead of just screen-only tools.

Right now my main focus is Terranova: the best Flipper Zero setup I can build, with clean firmware workflows, organized SD-card files, tested GPIO modules, and notes that separate useful tools from clutter.

I still run a personal cybersecurity home lab and build websites for local Springfield businesses, but the direction of this profile is Flipper Zero firmware, embedded hardware, and practical security gear that can be documented and improved over time.
